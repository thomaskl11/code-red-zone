Position limits & round strategy

What's set on ESPN's "Autopick Strategy" tab, and why.

PositionMinMaxWhy
Quarterback11League allows up to 4, but this is a single-QB league — a backup QB is a wasted pick, never worth the capital.
Running Back26League allows up to 8; 6 leaves room across the 5 bench spots for other positions, since no-PPR scoring rewards RB volume most.
Wide Receiver26Same reasoning as RB — enough depth without crowding out everything else.
Tight End13Matches the league's own max exactly — room for a streaming option beyond the starter.
Team D/ST11League allows up to 3, but one is enough — a 10-team league has deep enough waivers to stream byes instead of spending a pick.
Place Kicker11Same logic as D/ST — waivers cover bye weeks.

Every round of Pick-By-Pick Strategy is left on "Best Available" — no round is forced into a specific position. That's deliberate: the whole point of Consensus Arbitrage is following the rankings gap wherever it shows up, not deciding in advance that round 4 has to be a running back. The six caps above are the only guardrails; everything else follows the ranked queue below.
